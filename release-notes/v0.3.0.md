Since v0.2.1, link previews show the whole thing a link points to (#30). Hovering a link, or picking it with `p`, now shows the entire theorem, definition, equation or bibliography entry instead of a fixed-height crop, including the part that continues onto the next page. Index links find their term on the target page and show the definition it's in, including Japanese terms split across lines. Links inside a preview can be previewed in turn: hover them, or press `p` (or `f` to follow) while a preview is open, and the previews stack; `Esc` closes the top one. Clicking a link no longer leaves its hover preview behind.

## Features

- **Rendering:** tiled renderer with smooth motion, single-page and two-page spread modes (facing pages touch at the spine), auto-trimmed margins, and rounded page corners
- **Auto-reload:** documents reload when they change on disk, keep their state per document, and mark the regions that changed
- **SyncTeX:** forward and inverse search with Neovim
- **Navigation:** search, hints, marks, the jump list, a visual mode, and turning the page by scrolling past its edge in single-page mode
- **Link previews:** the whole theorem, definition, equation, bibliography entry or indexed term a link points to, across page breaks, in English and Japanese documents; links inside a preview open stacked previews, by hover or with `p` / `f`
- **Tabs and pickers:** tabs in the title bar, file and outline pickers, opening a PDF by typing its path, and ⌃^ to go back to the previous PDF
- **Appearance:** dark mode and dark paper, see-through and glass backgrounds with configurable style and tint, glass overlays, and a Liquid Glass app icon
- **Config file:** configurable keys, scroll and zoom steps, dark-mode colours, and corner radius; `g,` or `⌘,` opens it in your editor and saving applies it

## Install

rill is built from source; there's no prebuilt download. You need macOS 26 or later and Xcode 26 or later.

```sh
git clone https://github.com/ys-math/rill.git
cd rill
git checkout v0.3.0
make install
```

See the [README](https://github.com/ys-math/rill#install) for PATH setup and the full list of keys.
